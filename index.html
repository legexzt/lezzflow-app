How It Works

Hyperlocal commerce made effortless.

We bridge the gap between traditional corner shops and modern quick delivery through intelligent shelf sync and neighbourhood couriers.

STEP 01

Discover nearby stores

Find neighborhood kiranas, pharmacies, and specialty retailers within a 2km radius. View live store status, distances, and real-time inventory before stepping out.

Live stock visibility

For Sellers

Turn your retail shop into a high-speed digital powerhouse.

Empowering traditional shopkeepers and kirana owners with enterprise-grade tech. Zero tech headaches, zero hardware costs, and direct customer relationships.

Smart restock alerts

Predictive inventory notifications: “This item may run out before the weekend” so you never lose high-margin sales.

Hyperlocal demand insights

Zero-tech onboarding

Get your entire store online in 5 minutes using barcode scanning, voice search, or our ready-made regional catalog assistant.

Instant daily settlements

Zero holding periods. Every rupee earned settles directly into your UPI or bank account on the same day.
