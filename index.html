01 / ARCHITECTURE

How LezzFlow connects your street.

Three streamlined, transform-only engineering layers bridging local corner stores directly to your hands.

01 LOCAL RADAR

Discover verified nearby stores

Scan your immediate neighbourhood perimeter for verified kirana, pharmacies, and specialty retailers. Browse digital shelves synced directly with store inventory.

  • Sub-2.5km local radius scan
  • Real-time shelf stock confirmation
  • Zero artificial platform markup
02 DIRECT DISPATCH

Order directly from the shopkeeper

Place your basket in one tap. Your order routes instantly to the merchant’s terminal, where it is hand-picked and verified by the merchant who knows the products best.

  • Instant merchant screen notification
  • Digital substitutions with customer approval
  • Secure escrow payment flow
03 HYPERLOCAL TRANSIT

Doorstep arrival in minutes

A dedicated neighbourhood courier collects your order straight from the shop counter and delivers it directly to your door without warehousing or middle-mile delays.

  • Built for 10-minute neighbourhood delivery
  • Point-to-point direct dispatch routes
  • Zero dark stores, supporting real communities
