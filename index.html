THE FLOW

How LezzFlow connects your street.

Three streamlined layers bridging local corner stores directly to your hands.

01 LOCAL RADAR

Discover verified nearby stores

Scan your immediate neighbourhood for verified kiranas, pharmacies and specialty retailers. Browse digital shelves synced directly with store inventory.

  • Sub-2.5km local radius scan
  • Real-time shelf stock confirmation
  • Zero artificial platform markup
02 DIRECT DISPATCH

Order directly from the shopkeeper

Place your basket in one tap. Your order routes instantly to the merchant’s terminal, hand-picked and verified by the person who knows the products best.

  • Instant merchant screen notification
  • Smart substitutions with your approval
  • Secure UPI payment flow
03 HYPERLOCAL TRANSIT

Doorstep arrival in minutes

A dedicated neighbourhood courier collects your order straight from the shop counter and rides it directly to your door — no warehouses, no middle mile.

  • Built for 10-minute neighbourhood delivery
  • Point-to-point direct dispatch routes
  • Zero dark stores — real communities win
